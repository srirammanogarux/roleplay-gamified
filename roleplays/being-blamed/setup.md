# Setup · When a coworker blames you

Id `being-blamed` · game type **Being blamed** · scenario **The dropped handover** · fully scripted, like Asking for leave. Gate 1 draft. The script is `roleplays/being-blamed.js`; the assets are `media/being-blamed/assets.json`.

## 1. Pitch and people

Before your day off you handed an urgent request to Ryan, in writing. It never got done. On Monday, in front of your boss's boss, Ryan says it was never handed over properly. Fix the record with one calm fact, then get the room back to the fix.

- **Ryan** (coworker): easy-going, a bit lazy. Shifts the blame without thinking ("it was never handed over properly"). Backs down fast when he's calmly shown the fact; gets defensive if you attack him.
- **Elena** (your boss's boss): sharp, time-poor, hard to impress. She called the meeting, she asks you "is that right?", and she has the last word in every path. She cares about fixes, not blame.
- **Sarah** (coach): Guided path only, once, when you're about to lose.
- **The player** has no name. Nobody says one.

No Marcus in this one.

## 2. Situation and facts

It's Monday morning. The urgent request was due Friday. It never got done, the deadline was missed, and it cost the team. Elena has called you and Ryan into a small meeting room: "someone dropped this. what happened?" Ryan answers first: "honestly, it was never handed over properly." That's not true.

Facts the story gives you (you need these to win):
1. **Friday was your day off.** So the request had to go to Ryan.
2. **On Thursday at 5:10 PM you sent Ryan a written handover note:** what was done, what was left, and who to call.
3. **Ryan replied at 5:12 PM: "got it 👍".** The note and his reply are on your phone, and you can show them (the app draws them as a phone card).
4. **The fix:** you'll call the people waiting on it this morning and get it done by noon. Next time you'll go through a handover face to face, not only in writing.
5. **Elena cares about fixes, not blame.** This is the hint for how to win: one calm fact, then the plan.

## 4. Conversation pieces

**Room meter: two faces, each 0 to 100, high = on your side.** A face follows its number unless a turn sets it directly; that holds until the next turn.

| Face | Start | Moods (by value) | Turn overrides | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elena | 60 (55 after Ryan's opening) | disapproving below 20 · skeptical below 50 · neutral below 75 · interested from 75 | skeptical when she asks "is that right?", disapproving at the end of Got defensive | One calm fact lifts her a lot (60 to 76). Calling it a lie, attacking Ryan or digging up the past drops her fast. Apologies and vagueness drop her slowly. |
| Ryan | 25 (15 after his opening) | smug below 30 · surprised below 60 · sheepish from 60 | surprised, defensive, annoyed, casual-grin, sheepish | Smug while the blame sticks. Surprised, then sheepish, when the note lands. Defensive or annoyed if you attack him. Easy again (casual grin) if you let it slide. |

**You lose when** Elena drops below 20: she stops listening and decides you're the problem. Running out the clock is a separate, softer ending: Elena decides without your side and the blame sticks.

**Clock:** 60 seconds, like Leave's. When it runs out, Elena glances at her phone (her `checks-phone` clip), says *"okay. so the handover wasn't clear. let's make sure that doesn't happen again."* and moves on. Ending: IT STUCK TO YOU.

**Events (app-drawn cards):**
- `is-that-right`: right before Elena's turn 3 in every path, the room goes quiet and her face lifts to the centre (`turns-to-you`). Whatever you say next is the line that counts.
- `handover-note`: right after you cite the note (Perfect turn 4, the Guided hint response), a phone card pops in, drawn as a chat: your Thu 5:10 PM note, then Ryan's 5:12 PM "got it 👍".

**Sarah's hint (Guided only):** she comes in when Elena is at 22, close to losing.
- Hint: *"don't go over the past. just say when you handed it over, and what he wrote back."*
- Response you say: *"sorry, let me be clear. i left ryan a written handover on thursday at 5:10, and he replied "got it." i can show you."*
- Card: **Corrected it with one fact**
- Taking it gives star 2 and caps the run at 2 stars.

**Who speaks when:**
- Ryan opens with the blame line (his `shifts-blame` clip) and reacts after your replies.
- Elena asks "is that right?" (turn 3) and says the last line in every path.
- Sometimes two people talk in a row before your turn (Guided: Ryan, then Elena; Perfect: Ryan, then Elena).

**What the conversation screen must show (not a design):**
- Both faces at the top. Elena reads as the most important (she's the `lead`).
- Each face changes mood on its own.
- The person speaking is clearly marked, and each bubble shows whose line it is.
- A moment lifts the speaker's face to the centre, as today.
- The clock chip, the mic and the star cards stay as they are.
- The handover-note phone card pops in when the player cites it.

`defaultPath: 'defensive'` (like Leave's Poor). The player lines in `defensive`, `silent` and the bad turns of `guided` carry real learner mistakes: "i was hand over", "he never reads nothing", "you said me", "i was in leave, what i can do?", "i was not explain it good", "little bit confusion from both side", "soon as possible", "i was give him", "i explained him everything". The lines in `perfect`, the hint response and the good Guided turns are clean.

Beats for `perfect`:
1. Ryan: "honestly, it was never handed over properly."
2. You: "i hear you, ryan. can i add one thing?" (star 1)
3. Elena: "go ahead. is that right?" (event `is-that-right`)
4. You: the 5:10 note and his "got it", "i can show you." The card pops. (star 2)
5. Ryan, caught out: "oh... yeah, i did say that. i thought it could wait till monday."
6. Elena: "okay. so where does that leave us?"
7. You: "i'll call them this morning and get it done by noon. and next time, i'll go through the handover face to face." (star 3)
8. Ryan: "and i should've asked if it wasn't clear. i'll help you finish it."
9. Elena says the last line.

## 7. Open questions

1. **Stamps on wins.** Leave's results screen only stamps losses: red MISSION FAILED and amber TIME'S UP, with the text built into the screen. RECORD CORRECTED (gold) and CLOSE CALL (lavender) need the screen to take the stamp text and colour from each path. *Recommend:* yes. It's two fields per path, and the layout and animation stay as they are. Saying no's WEEKEND SAVED needs the same thing.
2. **Stayed silent: amber time's up with 1 star, or red fail with 0?** The script has amber with 1 star. *Recommend:* keep it. It matches Leave's Time's up, and it teaches that staying calm alone lets the blame stick.
3. **Story posters.** Saying no's scenes name a `poster` (the start still); this script's scenes don't. *Recommend:* add `poster: 'media/being-blamed/stills/story-N-<name>-start.png'` per scene once the stills are approved, so the first frame shows while the clip loads.
4. **The Monday message card.** Scene 2's card has Elena writing at 8:47 AM for a 9:00 meeting. *Recommend:* keep it. It's the only place the player sees she called the meeting, and it sets up "someone dropped this" in scene 3.
