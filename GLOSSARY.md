# Workplace Roleplays

Voice roleplays where an English learner talks their way through a hard moment at work, one spoken turn at a time, and is scored on how they came across and on their English.

## Roleplays and rules

**Roleplay**:
One workplace situation the learner plays through, named in plain English (e.g. "Asking for leave").
_Avoid_: Scenario, topic, level, episode, lesson

**Template**:
The fixed order of steps every roleplay follows: story, face-off, mission, conversation, results.
_Avoid_: Flow, format

**Game type**:
The rules for winning and losing a roleplay, defined by what makes you lose (e.g. Asking for something, Saying no, Confessing, Small talk, Being blamed). Each roleplay has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Mode, mechanic, genre

**Path**:
One scripted run through a roleplay's conversation, ending one particular way (e.g. Poor, Guided, Perfect, Time's up).
_Avoid_: Case, branch, variant

**Ending**:
How a path finishes, shown as a stamp on the results (e.g. MISSION FAILED, TIME'S UP, WEEKEND SAVED). Each game type decides its own endings.
_Avoid_: Outcome, result

**Roleplay file**:
The words and rules of one roleplay: its story, paths, meter values, stars, and the notes a live AI would need (personality, facts, star rules).
_Avoid_: Recipe, script, config

**Roleplay assets**:
The pictures, videos and voice lines a roleplay uses.
_Avoid_: Media, content

## Inside a roleplay

**Story**:
The short run of video scenes before the conversation that gives the learner the facts they'll need.
_Avoid_: Intro, context carousel, cutscene

**Face-off**:
The story beat that names who you're up against, or with (e.g. "YOU vs THE BOSS").
_Avoid_: Clash, versus screen

**Mission**:
The story's last screen: the goal, the three stars to earn, and the clock.
_Avoid_: Goal screen, brief

**Star**:
One of three things the learner should do in a roleplay; earning all three is the best result.
_Avoid_: Goal, objective, point

**Meter**:
The on-screen gauge of how the other person is reacting, which decides a loss when it hits its edge (e.g. Marcus's anger).
_Avoid_: Bar, gauge, health

**Clock**:
The in-story time limit on a conversation (e.g. Marcus's meeting in 5 minutes).
_Avoid_: Timer, countdown

**Moment**:
A short video where the character reacts big (greeting, anger, walking out), played over the conversation.
_Avoid_: Morph, takeover, reaction clip

**Hint**:
Sarah's one appearance, when the learner is about to lose; taking it caps the roleplay at 2 stars.
_Avoid_: Guided response, help, tip

## People

**Character**:
A person in the roleplays with a fixed name, face set, voice and personality that stay the same everywhere.
_Avoid_: Persona, NPC, avatar

**Player**:
The learner, speaking in their own voice; shown as a faceless silhouette.
_Avoid_: User (in story text), you-character

**Marcus**: the boss. **Sarah**: the guide. **Ryan**: a coworker. **Elena**: a senior leader, the boss's boss.

## Results

**How you came across**:
The first part of the results: scores for how the learner sounded to the other person (sure of themselves, to the point, calm), each with a line in the character's voice.
_Avoid_: Soft skills, composure

**Your English**:
The second part of the results: grammar, pronunciation and vocabulary, with the top fixes.
_Avoid_: Language score, accuracy

## Making assets

**Still**:
An approved image of a scene; every video starts and ends on stills.
_Avoid_: Keyframe, frame, thumbnail

**Take**:
One generated version of an asset; a new take never replaces an approved one until it's promoted.
_Avoid_: Version, retry

**Redo queue**:
The to-do list of review-page requests (redo, better prompt, replace, add scene) waiting to be generated.
_Avoid_: Backlog, tasks
